Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Worknote (the “App”) helps users record work hours, leave, pay settings, expense reimbursements, and receipts. This policy explains how information is handled.

Work and pay records, expense details, and receipt files are stored locally on your device. Worknote does not upload these records to its own server or synchronize them to an account.

1. Information stored in the App

Work shifts, leave, pay settings, reimbursement entries, notes, and attached receipts are stored in the App’s local device storage. The selected age group (under 13, 13–17, or 18+) is saved only on the device to determine whether ads may be shown. The App is intended for people aged 13 and older. Ads are not initialized or requested for users under 18.

If you export or share a receipt, the file is sent to the folder or third-party app you choose. That destination’s own privacy policy applies.

2. Advertising and third-party SDKs

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect or receive IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information. This data is handled under Google’s policies. Worknote does not send your work, pay, reimbursement records, or receipt files to the advertising SDK.

The App requests non-personalized ads. Google’s consent flow may appear where required. See Google’s Privacy Policy for more information.

3. Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them or clear the App’s data. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may delete local records. Exported or shared copies must be deleted separately from their destination.

4. Children and teens

Worknote is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users under 13 cannot proceed past the age screen. Users aged 13–17 may use the App without ads. Users aged 18 or older may receive ads according to applicable consent and advertising requirements.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated when the App’s functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version and effective date will be available on this page.
